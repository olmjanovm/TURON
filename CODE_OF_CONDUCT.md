# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Our standards
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Provide actionable, professional feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private/sensitive information.
- Disruptive or intentionally harmful behavior.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct.
